<div id="product_features">
	<p><b>HikaShop for Elementor</b> is a free WordPress plugin that lets you design your HikaShop store with <a href="https://elementor.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Elementor</a>, the page builder. Product listings, category listings, every part of the product page, the cart, the wishlist, the currency switcher and the filters become Elementor widgets you drag into your pages, and your product pages can be shown with a template you design in Elementor. It works with the free Elementor: nothing in it needs Elementor Pro.</p>

	<span class="blue_title">Features</span>
	<ul>
		<li><b>Products</b> widget: the products of a category (with or without its subcategories), products chosen by hand in the order you give, the products on sale, the products the visitor viewed last, or the products related to the product being viewed. You choose the order, the number of products, the columns, the product layout, and whether to show the price, the add to cart button, the quantity field, the wishlist button, the rating and the badges.</li>
		<li>A product picker that searches your catalog by name, code or id, for the products chosen by hand.</li>
		<li><b>Categories</b> widget: the subcategories of a category, with their number of products, the empty ones hidden if you want.</li>
		<li>Fourteen product page widgets: title, images, price, add to cart, description, variants, options, custom fields, specifications, dimensions, bundled products, files, rating and sharing. Place each one where you want in your product page template.</li>
		<li>One product page template for the whole shop, picked in Settings &gt; HikaShop for Elementor. While you design it, the latest product of your shop stands in, so you see real content in the editor.</li>
		<li>The variant selectors, the add to cart, the image gallery and the links to a given variant work as on HikaShop's own product page. Unpublished products and products hidden from the visitor's user group or language keep HikaShop's own answer.</li>
		<li><b>Cart</b>, <b>Wishlist</b>, <b>Currency switcher</b> and <b>Filters</b> widgets: HikaShop's own modules, with their full settings one click away from the Elementor panel.</li>
		<li>A style tab on each widget (colors, typography, alignment), on top of HikaShop's own CSS.</li>
		<li>Nothing of the shop is redone: prices, taxes, discounts, stock, access rights, the cart and the layout overrides of your theme apply as on HikaShop's own pages.</li>
		<li>Open source (GPL), on <a href="https://github.com/hikashop-nicolas/hikashop-for-elementor" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub</a>.</li>
	</ul>

	<span class="blue_title">How it works</span>
	<ul>
		<li>1. Install and activate Elementor and HikaShop, then this plugin.</li>
		<li>2. Edit a page with Elementor and search the widget panel for "HikaShop".</li>
		<li>3. For the product pages, create a template in Templates &gt; Saved Templates, place the product widgets in it, and choose it in Settings &gt; HikaShop for Elementor.</li>
	</ul>

<div id="product_use"><span class="blue_title">Use</span>
	<ul>
		<li>Free plugin, for every HikaShop edition, Starter included (the Filters widget needs HikaShop Business, the edition with the filters)</li>
		<li>Requires HikaShop 6.8.2 minimum, a package downloaded since 12 November 2026</li>
		<li>Requires Elementor 3.24 minimum, the free plugin is enough</li>
		<li>Compatible with WordPress 6.6 minimum and PHP 7.4 minimum</li>
		<li>WordPress only (Elementor does not exist on Joomla)</li>
		<li>No time limit</li>
		<li>Unlimited usage</li>
	</ul>
</div>
